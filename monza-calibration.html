What this establishes

This is a provisional track-target comparison, not a trained driver model. Fuel, setup, traffic and energy instructions are unknown. The simulation uses an isolated medium-tire lap after warm-up; reference laps include medium and soft compounds. Lap-time agreement alone cannot identify aerodynamic coefficients. Boundary samples may be held for at most 0.35 s; FP2 lap 9 uses a 0.093 s end hold. FP3 lap 2 was excluded because its time was deleted. Leclerc’s race was unsuitable because no complete flying laps were available.

Source: OpenF1 public telemetry (~3.7 Hz; brake is binary, not pressure). Raw records, request URLs, timestamps and SHA-256 hashes are saved under docs/research/monza. Gameplay reference: EA’s 2026 Season Pack developer interview. No EA engine data or assets are used.
