# Wheel axles and straight-line racing · 27 October 2026

The first steering-animation fix did not address baked wheel camber. The supplied wheel meshes have approximately 2–4 degrees of source tilt. Rotating them about a horizontal local axis made that tilt rotate with the tyre, producing visible precession even with constant steering. Simply damping steering cannot solve this geometry problem.

The runtime adapter now fits each source tyre's axle from its covariance, de-cambers a cached copy of the rim/brake geometry, and applies fixed camber outside the axle-spin rotation. Tyres use a concentric, rounded, 96-segment slick envelope with the source width and documented radius. The fixed camber and source rim details remain visible. A support calculation keeps each cambered tyre in contact with the shared road. This replaces the coarse source tyre surface, not the body or downloaded GLB file. Original buffers remain immutable; teammates share corrected buffers. These are display approximations, not exact tyre CAD or a suspension simulation.

New `monza-3d-6` races use a smoothly blended longer steering preview on open straights (0.20–0.36 seconds of forward travel). Bends and wheel-to-wheel overlap retain the close controller response. This makes lane changes more progressive without suppressing attacks. Tire/energy/pace controls, aerodynamic and regulatory assumptions, pit rules and 10 ms physics substeps are unchanged. Old model-5 saves retain their original driving policy, with a genuine pre-change continuation fixture proving exact continuation; all saves receive the corrected visual wheel assembly.

Validation: all 116 tests pass, including 72 wheel/detail combinations through complete revolutions, fixed axle/camber, less than 1 mm silhouette runout and contact gap, buffer sharing/immutability, a progressive lane change, a completed side-by-side pass without contact, original model-4/model-5 continuation, and existing playback/save/energy/contact checks. TypeScript passes. The wheel tests inspect transformed tyre vertices, rather than just the pivot direction.

One paired full-distance seed-2026 comparison finished all 12 cars in both models, with valid fuel, tires and battery. Model 5: 3 contacts, 11 recorded position-change events, 5160.40 s until the race completed. Model 6: 1 contact, 4 position-change events, 5147.65 s. These events are controller outcomes, not proof of a calibrated overtaking frequency or guaranteed improvement across seeds. The new controller completed the separate controlled passing test.
