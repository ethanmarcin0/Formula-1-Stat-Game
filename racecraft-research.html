Shared braking calibration

We sampled the fastest five timed laps for each Ferrari and McLaren driver in FP2 and FP3. Seven failed quality filters. The model was compared against 20 FP2 laps; 13 FP3 laps were reserved for validation. The selected value is a speed-planning parameter, not a claim about the car's maximum braking force.
